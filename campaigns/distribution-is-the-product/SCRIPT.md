# "THE CURRENT": Hero Film Script
**Campaign:** Distribution is the Product · **Brand:** Default · **Length:** 90s · **Ratio:** 2.39:1 master, 1.43:1 IMAX-style crops for the history beats

**Grammar:**
- **B&W** is the Industrial Race (objective, history).
- **COLOR** is the Intelligence Race (now).
- The **TICK** never stops until the last frame.

**VO:** One voice, low and unhurried, like a man testifying. Never a "trailer voice".
