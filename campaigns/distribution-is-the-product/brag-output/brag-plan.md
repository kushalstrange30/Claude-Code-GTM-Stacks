# /brag plan: "THE CURRENT" (25s cut)

**Project:** *Distribution is the Product*, a launch campaign for Default (default.com)
**Tone:** `cinematic`, with freeform direction "Oppenheimer-style, heroic, a ticking clock, history vs now"
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080 · 30fps · **25.0s**
**Music/SFX:** on. An original score is synthesized for this cut: tick clock, drone, Shepard rise, hits. Nothing is licensed.
**Voice:** off. The supers carry the script.

## Planning rubric

1. **What is it?** Default, one GTM system that runs inbound, waterfall enrichment, qualification, routing and scheduling, with agents (Dot) orchestrating.
2. **Who is it for?** AI/B2B SaaS founders, RevOps leaders and CROs who are losing deals to faster competitors.
3. **What's the one claim?** In the AI race, nobody remembers who built it first. They remember who got there first. *Distribution is the product.*
4. **What's the proof/story?** Edison. The bulb was the easy part. He built the wire first: Pearl Street 1882, 82 customers. In 1908 he owned the film stock, the reels and the theaters. The one time he lost (AC vs DC), he lost to a current that traveled *farther*.
5. **What does the product look like?** A form submission flows FORM → ENRICH → QUALIFY → ROUTE → BOOKED on a dark canvas, with a stopwatch.
6. **What's the hook (0–2s)?** Black. A single tick. A filament ignites. *"Everyone remembers the bulb."*
7. **What's the turn?** The jump from black and white to color, at the switchboard: *"Every era has a switchboard."*
8. **What's the payoff?** The clock goes silent on *DISTRIBUTION IS THE PRODUCT.*
9. **What must not happen?** No generic SaaS language. Edison's name is never printed on screen. Weapons are cropped out of ref 3 (ref 3 is cut from this edit for length; it stays in the 90s hero). The "9 seconds" is labeled as a dramatization.

## Music cue guidance
There's no bundled track. The score is generated from the same tick map the visuals read (`assets/ticks.json`), so beat sync is exact by construction. Tempo runs from 60 → ~165 bpm (0.25s → 20.35s), with a dropped beat at the AC twist, calm ticks at 20.9 and 21.7, the hit at 22.40, and silence from 22.6 to 23.9.
