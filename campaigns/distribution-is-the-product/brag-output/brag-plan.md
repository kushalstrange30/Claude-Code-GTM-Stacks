# /brag plan: "THE CURRENT" (60s, product-led cut)

**Project:** *Distribution is the Product*, a launch campaign for Default (default.com)
**Tone:** `cinematic` → `app-store` clarity for the product act. Oppenheimer-style setup, then the product carries the film.
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080 · 30fps · **60.0s** (the user asked for 60s, overriding /brag's default 15–25s)
**Split:** History **15.6s (26%)** → Default in action **31.5s** + system **4.5s** + payoff **8.4s** = **74% product and brand**
**Music/SFX:** an original synthesized score: a history tick clock, then a 120 bpm pulse with chords, a UI cue on every product action, a Shepard rise, the hit and silence.
**Voice:** off.

## Planning rubric

1. **What is it?** Default, one GTM platform: website intent, forms, waterfall enrichment, AI qualification, routing, scheduling, workflows (Slack/CRM) and Dot, the revenue agent.
2. **Who is it for?** AI and B2B SaaS founders, RevOps leaders and CROs.
3. **What's the one claim?** In the AI race, the product isn't what you build. It's how fast you reach the buyer. *Distribution is the product.*
4. **What's the proof?** Edison as the setup: the bulb was easy, he built the wire. Then one inbound lead goes from anonymous visitor to booked meeting, live in Default.
5. **What does the product look like?** A persistent app window, with the sidebar lighting the active module, playing through seven product beats.
6. **What's the hook?** A tick, a filament, *"Everyone remembers the bulb."*
7. **What's the turn?** The switchboard flare into color: *"2026 · Yours runs on Default."*
8. **What's the payoff?** *Not eight tools. One current.* → *Anyone can build the bulb.* → **DISTRIBUTION IS THE PRODUCT.**
9. **Guardrails:** Edison is never named on screen. The stopwatch is labeled "DRAMATIZATION". Customer, company and rep names are fictional. Data providers are shown as "Source 1–4", and no competitors are named.
